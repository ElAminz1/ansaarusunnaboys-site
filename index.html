About the school

ASA is an Islamic boys' boarding school that nurtures disciplined, confident and responsible young men, academically prepared and grounded in Islamic values. We combine academic education, character development, student leadership, Islamic learning and structured boarding.

Academic Excellence

Purposeful teaching, assessment, progress tracking and preparation across Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel, CBC and 8-4-4 pathways.

Character & Discipline

Responsibility, respect, leadership and self-management through clear routines and positive student guidance.

Faith & Values

Qur'an, Hadith, Madarasa and Islamic studies integrated into daily student life.

Knowledge with adab.

Islamic learning is part of every day at ASA. Our programme builds students' Islamic knowledge, character and conduct, so they grow into young men who know their deen and live it.
