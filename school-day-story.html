A day at Ansaaru Sunna

Follow one boy from the first call to Fajr to lights out. Academics, Madarasa and prayer share the same day, in this order.

Scroll to begin

05:00Fajr in congregation

The day starts with prayer

The boys wake before sunrise, make wudhu and pray Fajr together. The morning adhkar follow before anyone opens a book.

06:15Qur'an halaqa

Qur'an and Hadith, in small groups

Each boy recites to his halaqa teacher, who records where he is. The progress is kept on the school portal for his parents.

07:30Breakfast

Breakfast, then assembly

A hot meal in the dining hall, then the whole school gathers for assembly and the day's announcements.

08:30CBC8-4-4IGCSE

Lessons in three curricula

CBC from Grade 5 to Grade 11, 8-4-4 in Form 3, and IGCSE from Year 7 to Year 10 with Cambridge and Edexcel. Every class has its own timetable and subject teachers.

13:00Dhuhr and lunch

Pause, pray, eat

The boys pray Dhuhr in congregation and have lunch. The afternoon is Madarasa time: Arabic, Fiqh, Tawheed and Sirah.

15:30AsrGames

Asr, then out in the field

After Asr the boys play football and other sports, or join their clubs and contests. A tired boy studies better.

18:30Maghrib and dinner

Maghrib, then dinner

Prayer first, then dinner together. The boarding staff take attendance and check that every boy is present.

20:30Evening prep

Supervised study

After Isha the boys do their prep in the classrooms with a teacher present, and revise what they covered that day.

22:00Lights out

Lights out, ready for Fajr

Dormitories are quiet, and the boys sleep early. Tomorrow starts again at the call to prayer.
